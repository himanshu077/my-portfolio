# Jeemba

**Creator Commerce & Video Shopping Marketplace**

The client needed a creator-driven eCommerce marketplace connecting shoppers, content creators and administrators, with video-based product discovery, affiliate commission tracking, secure payments and central marketplace management.

---

## What I built

A full-stack marketplace with three role-based dashboards:

- **Creators** upload short-form videos, tag products, build collections and earn affiliate commissions.
- **Shoppers** discover products through creator videos, manage their cart and check out securely with Stripe.
- **Administrators** manage creators, products, orders, content moderation and analytics.

## Outcome

A production-ready creator-commerce platform combining social shopping, automated commission attribution, secure checkout and streamlined marketplace operations.
