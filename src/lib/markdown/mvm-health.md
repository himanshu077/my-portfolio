# MVM Health

**AI Lead Qualification & Patient Engagement for a Multi-Location Vein Clinic**

A growing multi-location vein treatment clinic in the United States was generating hundreds of inquiries a month from Google Ads, Facebook lead forms, website contact forms, landing pages and phone-call requests. Responses were slow outside business hours, front-desk staff spent their time on repetitive questions, unqualified leads slipped through, and follow-up was inconsistent across locations.

---

## What I built

- **AI sales agent** that engages every new lead 24/7
- **Automated lead qualification** before a lead reaches the front desk
- **Multi-location routing** so each patient lands at the right clinic
- **SMS & email automation** for follow-up and patient re-engagement campaigns
- **CRM pipeline optimisation** and a reporting & analytics dashboard

## Outcome

By combining AI-powered conversations with CRM automation, the clinic moved its lead management from a largely manual workflow to a scalable patient-acquisition system.
